## 1. Before you start looking
- Set your maximum total budget, not just the purchase price
- Allow for stamp duty/transfer fees, registration and insurance
- Estimate ongoing fuel, servicing, tyres and maintenance costs
- Check the car is suitable for your needs
- If you're a P-plater, check the vehicle is legal for you to drive
- Get an insurance quote before committing to a particular car
- Compare the asking price against similar vehicles

## 2. Check the car's history
- Get the car's VIN
- Confirm the VIN matches the actual vehicle
- Complete a PPSR check
- Check whether the vehicle has been reported stolen
- Check whether it has been written off
- Check whether there is finance owing/security interest
- Review the vehicle's odometer history, where available
- Review its service and maintenance history

## 3. Check the seller
- Confirm the seller's identity
- Confirm they have the right to sell the vehicle
- Check that the seller's details are consistent with the vehicle records
- Be cautious if you're pressured to pay a deposit immediately
- Never rely solely on an advertisement, text messages or photos

## 4. Inspect the car
- Arrange an independent pre-purchase inspection
- Check body panels for dents, scratches and previous repairs
- Look for inconsistent paint or panel gaps
- Inspect tyres for tread, damage and uneven wear
- Check the windscreen and other glass
- Check headlights, indicators and brake lights
- Check the interior, seatbelts and controls
- Start the engine and check for dashboard warning lights
- Check for unusual noises, smoke, leaks or smells
- Confirm the odometer reading
- Check the VIN again against your vehicle history/PPSR records

## 5. Test drive
- Test the brakes
- Check steering and handling
- Listen for unusual noises or vibration
- Check acceleration and transmission operation
- Make sure the vehicle doesn't pull noticeably to one side
- Test air conditioning and important electrical equipment
- If something doesn't feel right, have it investigated before buying

## 6. Before you pay
- Agree on the final purchase price in writing
- Confirm exactly what is included — vehicle, keys, accessories, etc.
- Confirm how any outstanding finance will be paid out
- Use a secure payment method
- Independently verify payment details before transferring money
- Don't transfer funds simply because the seller is creating urgency
- Obtain a written sale agreement/receipt

- Receive all keys
- Receive relevant service records/manuals
- Record the vehicle's condition at handover
- Complete the required registration transfer
- Make sure your insurance is effective before driving away

## 8. Once you own it
- Know the manufacturer's recommended tyre pressures
- Check tyre pressure and condition regularly
- Know what the major dashboard warning lights mean
- Check relevant fluids according to the manufacturer's instructions
- Follow the manufacturer's service schedule
- Have unusual braking, steering, noises or vibrations investigated
- Keep registration and insurance current
