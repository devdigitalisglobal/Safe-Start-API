## Documents to check before buying
- Registration details — confirm the vehicle is currently registered
- VIN — check the VIN on the vehicle matches the registration and other documents
- PPSR certificate — check for a security interest/finance owing and whether the vehicle is recorded as stolen or written off
- Vehicle history report — review available history, including odometer and other vehicle records
- Service history — check logbooks, digital service records and maintenance invoices
- Roadworthy/safety inspection — check whether one is required for the transaction in your state or territory
- Seller identification — verify who you are buying the vehicle from
- Finance payout information — if money is owing, establish how the existing loan/security interest will be discharged

## Documents to get when you buy
- Sale agreement showing the buyer, seller, vehicle and agreed purchase price
- Receipt/proof of payment
- Registration transfer documents or confirmation
- PPSR certificate from your pre-purchase search
- Finance payout/discharge confirmation, if applicable
- Service books and maintenance records
- Owner's manual and other vehicle documentation supplied with the car
- Inspection report, if you had a pre-purchase inspection completed
- Details of any warranty that applies to the vehicle

## Documents to organise before driving away
- Driver's licence is current and appropriate for the vehicle
- Registration is current
- Required CTP/compulsory injury insurance is in place according to your state or territory's system
- Your chosen car insurance is active from the appropriate time
- Registration transfer has been lodged/completed as required
- Roadside assistance membership/details, if you have cover

## Documents to keep after purchase
Keep these together digitally or in a safe place:
- Purchase/sale agreement
- Payment receipt
- PPSR certificate
- Registration records
- Insurance policy and insurer contact details
- Service history
- Maintenance and repair invoices
- Inspection reports
- Tyre/battery receipts and warranties
- Finance documents, if financed
- Roadside assistance details
